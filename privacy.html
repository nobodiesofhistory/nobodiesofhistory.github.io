Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

"Nobodies of History Uploader" is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Nobodies of History YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.
  • It accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's explicit consent, to upload videos, thumbnails and captions.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or any third party.
  • Authorization can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service (youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
